Investment Opportunities
What types of investment opportunities does the First Mover Fund offer?

First Mover Fund focuses on early-stage land restoration and environmental projects that generate both agricultural and environmental commodities, particularly carbon credits purchased at discounted rates. We connect private investors with these opportunities before institutional capital arrives — capturing the asymmetric upside that comes with early-stage positioning.

How does the company assess potential returns?

Our team evaluates projected revenue from commodities generated by projects, including carbon credit sales, crop yields, and other agricultural outputs. We consider project scale, duration, and market conditions through detailed financial modelling, stress-testing assumptions against a range of market scenarios to provide investors with a realistic range of potential outcomes.

What strategies manage investment risks?

Risk mitigation at First Mover Fund involves prioritising projects that meet international standards, diversifying across regions and ecosystems, engaging local communities in project execution, implementing insurance mechanisms where available, and maintaining strong governance frameworks. No investment is without risk, but our multi-layered approach is designed to protect capital at each stage.

What vetting process is followed for new projects?

Our process includes initial screening, full documentation review, field visits, stakeholder interviews, financial analysis, risk assessment, third-party verification, additionality assessment, monitoring protocol review, and legal compliance verification. Only projects that pass every stage of this process reach our investors.

Who would be a typical investor in First Mover Fund?

Our investors include high-net-worth accredited individuals, family offices, investment professionals, institutional investors, corporate investors, financial institutions, and sovereign wealth funds. All investors must meet SEC accreditation criteria, with a minimum investment of $200,000 and a 5–7 year investment horizon.

Carbon Credits & Markets
What are carbon credits?

Each carbon credit represents one tonne of CO2 either avoided or removed from the atmosphere through a verified project. Credits are listed on recognised registries such as Verra or Gold Standard, then sold to organisations seeking to address their carbon footprint. They are a regulated, verifiable commodity with growing institutional demand.

What drives demand for carbon credits?

Demand is driven by corporate emission reduction targets, growing investor and consumer pressure for sustainability, regulatory requirements including CSRD and CORSIA, and government climate commitments under the Paris Agreement. Morgan Stanley projects the voluntary carbon market will grow from approximately $2 billion in 2022 to $100 billion by 2030.

What reasons support investing in carbon credits?

The case for carbon credit investment includes significant market growth projections, strong regulatory tailwinds, high potential returns from early-stage entry, portfolio diversification from conventional asset classes, measurable environmental and social impact, improvements to underlying land productivity, and potential tax incentives in various jurisdictions.

How does First Mover Fund participate in carbon markets?

We invest in early-stage environmental projects that generate carbon credits through reforestation and ecological restoration. By entering projects before third-party certification, we secure portions of future credit production at discounted pricing — with the intention of selling certified credits at market rates as projects reach validation milestones.

How does the company ensure carbon credit credibility?

We engage rigorous verification and certification processes with reputable third-party organisations to verify that projects meet established international standards. This includes additionality assessments, permanence monitoring, and ongoing reporting. Only credits from properly certified projects are sold, enhancing both transparency and market value.

Environmental Impact
What benefits do nature-based solutions provide?

Nature-based solutions deliver a range of measurable benefits: carbon sequestration, land restoration that supports local farmers, biodiversity conservation, sustainable community development, and co-benefits including improved water quality, reduced soil erosion, and enhanced disaster resilience. These outcomes make our projects attractive to both financial and impact-focused investors.

What specific environmental impact does the company target?

Our investments focus on reforestation, ecological restoration, and land restoration initiatives that contribute to measurable carbon sequestration, farmer livelihoods, and ecosystem health. We target projects in regions where ecological degradation has created the greatest opportunity for restoration — and where land can be acquired at a significant discount to its restored value.

How are environmental outcomes measured and reported?

We track carbon sequestration rates, biodiversity indicators, and the overall health of restored ecosystems through regular third-party assessments and reporting. Investors receive periodic updates on both financial performance and verified environmental metrics, consistent with international reporting standards.
